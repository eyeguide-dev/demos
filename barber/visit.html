Visit and FAQ

218 Atlantic Avenue, Brooklyn, NY 11201. Street parking on Atlantic Avenue; the A, C, G and 2, 3 trains are a short walk.

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes, whenever a chair is free. Booking is the safest way to avoid a wait.

How do I book?

Call or text (208) 485-0744 any time. Our AI receptionist answers, offers open times for the barber you ask for, and puts you on the calendar. It can bring in a person if you ask.

Can I book a specific barber?

Yes. Each barber has their own chair and their own schedule, so say who you want and a time of day.

What payment do you take?

Cash, cards and tap to pay. Prices are the same whichever barber you book.

How late can I be?

Ten minutes. After that we may need to move you to the next opening.

Cancellations?

Please tell us at least two hours ahead so someone else can have the time.
